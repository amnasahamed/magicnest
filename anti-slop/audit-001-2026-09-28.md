# Magic Nest Readability Audit 001

Date: 2026-09-28

Scope: Homepage, Pre-KG, LKG, and UKG pages at 320, 390, 768, 1024, and 1366 CSS pixels. Checks covered settled visual appearance, text sizing, clipping, horizontal overflow, touch targets, core color contrast, and a 200% text-resize stress test.

## Verdict

The main sales journey is readable on standard phones and laptops, but the site is not yet a perfect readability pass. Primary headlines, body copy, calls to action, and imagery remain clear. The remaining risks are concentrated in small supporting text, three low-contrast color treatments, undersized secondary navigation targets, and text resizing.

## Findings

1. **HIGH, normal-sized accent text fails WCAG AA contrast.** `brand-yellow` on the secondary teal background measures about 3.86:1, `brand-teal` on the light background measures about 3.03:1, and 80% muted text on the footer background measures about 2.96:1. Normal text requires 4.5:1. These treatments appear in supporting labels, badges, course information, and footer headings. This makes useful sales details harder to scan in bright light or for users with lower vision. Violates R-25.

2. **HIGH, supporting sales and curriculum copy is too small.** At phone widths, the audit found real visible text styles at 10 and 11 pixels, plus many details at 12 pixels. Examples include book-inclusion labels, optional-language badges, textbook benefits, course-stage labels, and book curriculum points. The homepage contains 14 elements below 12 pixels. The Pre-KG page contains 22, and the LKG and UKG pages contain 44 each, including nested duplicate detections. Even where the copy is technically legible, it is not comfortable for parents scanning on a phone. Violates R-03, R-06, and R-35.

3. **HIGH, some secondary navigation targets are too short for touch.** The `Programs` or `All programs` link on each syllabus page is approximately 20 pixels high. The six desktop navigation links are approximately 20 to 21 pixels high at 1024 pixels and above. Primary mobile buttons pass, but these secondary links do not meet the recommended 44 by 44 pixel touch area. Violates R-03 and R-35.

4. **HIGH, the 200% text-resize stress test exposes content beyond the viewport.** The homepage does not create document-level horizontal scrolling because overflow is hidden, but parts of the relationship section extend to about 440 pixels inside a 390-pixel viewport when the root text size is doubled. Hiding the overflow masks rather than resolves the reflow problem. Violates R-35 and C-4.

## Confirmed passes

- No document-level horizontal overflow at 320, 390, 768, 1024, or 1366 pixels on any tested page.
- No visible text clipping at standard text size on any tested page.
- Primary headings and main body copy remain readable at both phone and laptop widths.
- Homepage phone controls all meet the 44-pixel touch-target minimum.
- The mobile navigation uses a clearly labelled `Menu` control instead of an icon alone.
- Primary buttons are 48 pixels or taller and remain easy to tap.
- Core text combinations pass AA: foreground on background 12.27:1, muted body text on background 5.52:1, primary button text 8.02:1, and light text on secondary 6.03:1.
- The sticky mobile trial button reserves space and does not cover the final page content.
- Content reflows into single-column phone layouts without card collisions.

## Conversion assessment

The homepage's primary message and calls to action are strong enough to understand and use on a phone. The weakness begins when a parent reads supporting proof and detailed curriculum information. Those details help justify the purchase, so making them small or low-contrast reduces confidence at the exact point where the parent is evaluating value.

Recommended launch status: acceptable for a soft launch, but fix findings 1 through 4 before paid promotion or a high-traffic campaign.
